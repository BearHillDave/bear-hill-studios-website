About Laura

I began my career as an actor, training at the Bristol Old Vic Theatre School. During my time in Bristol, I worked with Nick Park and Peter Lord on Chicken Run, voicing additional chicken characters and recording guide tracks for Julia Sawalha and Jane Horrocks. It turned out I was quite good at impersonating them!

My voice work went on to include the original London Olympic cable car announcements, wildlife documentaries for BBC Studios Natural History, BBC Radio 4 plays and a range of radio ads and narration.

I took a break from acting to have a family and re train as a speech and language therapist and began working with people who had experienced brain injury.

Today, I balance my time between family life, voice acting, our family production company, and developing voice therapy as my specialist area within speech and language therapy.

Whether I'm helping someone find their voice or bringing a story to life, this is the work that makes me happy.

If you'd like to discuss a project, get in touch and we'll take it from there.
