Scroll Reveal

Classes

.reveal → opacity:0, translateY(24px)
.reveal.visible → opacity:1, none
.d1 → delay 0.08s
.d2 → delay 0.16s
.d3 → delay 0.24s
.d4 → delay 0.32s

Rule

Never put .reveal on both a parent
and its children — the parent's
opacity:0 hides children regardless
of their own state.
