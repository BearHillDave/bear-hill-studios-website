About Laura

A voice built on experience

Laura began her career training at the Bristol Old Vic Theatre School, working with Nick Park and Peter Lord on Chicken Run — voicing additional chicken characters and recording guide tracks for Julia Sawalha and Jane Horrocks.

Her subsequent career spanned the original London Olympic cable car announcements, wildlife documentaries for BBC Studios Natural History, BBC Radio 4 plays, and a wide range of radio ads and narration.

Today, Laura balances voice acting with a clinical practice as a Speech and Language Therapist specialising in voice therapy — a unique combination that makes her an exceptional coach as well as performer.

Laura Cox

Voice Coaching

Combining professional acting experience with clinical Speech and Language Therapy — a uniquely informed approach to vocal development.

My Approach

Where performance meets clinical expertise

I trained as an actor at the Bristol Old Vic Theatre School and spent over ten years working professionally in the industry. Five years ago, I retrained as a Speech and Language Therapist at City, University of London, deepening my understanding of the voice from a clinical and technical perspective.

Alongside this, I co-founded Bear Hill Studios with my husband, creating immersive audiobooks for children, and I continue to work professionally as a voice artist from our home studio in Bath.

My combined experience as a professional voice user and a Speech and Language Therapist allows me to support performers with both vocal technique and vocal hygiene. I understand the demands placed on the voice in performance and can help you develop sustainable techniques to improve your voice, speech, and confidence.

What I offer

Vocal Technique

Breath support, resonance, projection and range — building on solid foundations.

Vocal Hygiene

Practical guidance on protecting your voice for sustainable long-term use.

Speech & Clarity

Articulation, diction and accent work tailored to your goals.

Performance Confidence

Finding and owning your voice for auditions, presentations and broadcasts.

Dave Cox

Sound & Interaction Design

Nearly 30 years of experience in creative technology, interactive design, and immersive audio — united by a belief that sound and experience are inseparable.

About Dave

The thinking behind the sound

Dave co-founded Lean Mean Fighting Machine in 2004 — a digital agency later acquired by M&C Saatchi. Since then he has developed products, produced audio, created brand identities, designed UX, developed XR experiences and worked in illustration and animation.

His guiding belief: there is a continuous line between sound, music, interaction and animation. All of it is about judging timing, weight, flow, space, surprise and balance.

Selected Work

A career built on immersion

Lean Mean Fighting Machine

Co-founded one of the UK's most creative digital agencies in 2004. Led interactive and brand work for major clients before the agency was acquired by M&C Saatchi.

Melody VR — Rescape Innovation

Spent several years specialising in Virtual Reality, becoming Product Director and designer for Melody VR — a clinically trialled wellbeing experience developed with Cardiff University that used music in VR to reduce anxiety. Results from the large-scale alpha trial were extremely positive.

Catmasonic — Important Small Things

Bear Hill Studios produced the voiceover for Catmasonic, made by former Rescape colleagues now at Important Small Things. A perfect example of how audio and interaction expertise combine.

Bear Hill Studios

Today Dave brings all of his experience to bear on creating immersive audiobooks and sound design for games, XR and digital media — always asking how sound can deepen the experience.
