## 3. Fix first

1. **DKIM record is at the wrong DNS name** (Netlify DNS panel). The `v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=…` TXT record is published on the apex `bearhillstudios.co.uk`, and `google._domainkey.bearhillstudios.co.uk` returns NXDOMAIN (checked against the local resolver, 1.1.1.1 and 8.8.8.8). This means Google Workspace mail is not DKIM-verifiable. Fix: move the TXT record to host `google._domainkey`, then click "Start authentication" in Google Admin.
2. **No meta description and no Open Graph/Twitter tags on any page** (`index.html:5` and the same spot in every page's `<head>`). Fix: add `<meta name="description">`, `og:title`/`og:description`/`og:image`/`og:url`/`og:type`, `twitter:card`, plus `<link rel="canonical">` pointing at `https://bearhillstudios.co.uk/…`.
3. **The MP3 download links go nowhere** (`href="#" download`). They are at `index.html:344, 419`, `work.html:272, 536, 633` and `laura.html:381, 411, 442, 470, 498, 529, 560`. Fix: point each link at its player's `data-src` file, or remove the link.
4. **The contact form shows "Message sent" even when sending fails** (`contact.html:460`). The `fetch` result is never checked and errors are swallowed in `catch (_) {}`. With `novalidate` (`contact.html:337`) and no JS validation, an empty form also "succeeds". Fix: call `form.reportValidity()` before sending, check `res.ok`, and show an error state when it fails.
5. **Low-contrast red labels and no reduced-motion support** (`main.css:723`, `main.css:654`, `main.css:524`). The red `.wi-eyebrow` text (#cf2a0a, 12px) has a 3.15:1 contrast ratio on #211e1a, below the 4.5:1 AA minimum. The site also has no `prefers-reduced-motion` handling for the reveal animations, parallax or the bouncing arrow. Fix: lighten the red used for text (or make the labels larger and bold), and add a `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block that turns off `.reveal` transitions, the parallax transform and the `scrollArrow` animation.

## 4. Full findings

### SEO
- ❌ No `<meta name="description">` on any of the 7 pages (verified: 0 matches).
- ⚠️ No `<link rel="canonical">`. The `bear-hill-studios-site.netlify.app` subdomain serves the same content with a 200 status, so search engines see duplicate content.
- ⚠️ `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` are missing (the live site returns 404 for both).
- ⚠️ `lang="en"` should be `en-GB` for a UK business.
- ⚠️ There's no branded `404.html`, so Netlify shows its default page.
- ✅ Every page has a unique `<title>`.
- ✅ `www` → apex and http → https both redirect with 301.
- ℹ️ No JSON-LD structured data (LocalBusiness / Organization). Optional.

### Social
- ❌ No `og:title` / `og:image` on any page, so shared links show no preview card.
- ⚠️ No `og:description`, `og:url`, `og:type` or `twitter:card`.
- ⚠️ No `apple-touch-icon`. ℹ️ No `theme-color` and no web manifest.

### Accessibility
- ❌ Red eyebrow text has a 3.15:1 contrast ratio (`main.css:723`, `.wi-eyebrow`, 12px). Needs 4.5:1.
- ⚠️ Muted `--cream-50` text has a 4.52:1 ratio on plain `--light-black`, which only just passes AA. Over the lighter areas of the `mix-blend-mode: lighten` blur layer it probably drops below 4.5:1. Affected: `.wi-yt-link` and other muted links/labels at 10.7px, `.cta-v2-body`, `.footer-v2-cookies`, `.wi-player-subtitle`.
- ⚠️ Form placeholders use `--cream-20`, which is 1.8:1 (`contact.html:87`). The fields have proper `<label>`s, so this is minor.
- ⚠️ No `prefers-reduced-motion` handling anywhere (verified: 0 matches in `main.css`, the HTML or the JS).
- ⚠️ Contact form inputs set `outline: none` (`contact.html:78`), and the only focus cue is the underline changing from cream-20 to cream-50. That change is too faint to work as a focus indicator. `main.css` has no `:focus-visible` styles at all, so everything else relies on browser defaults.
- ℹ️ No skip-to-content link.
- ✅ The mobile menu opens and closes on all 7 pages. Focus trapping with `inert` is in place per `CLAUDE.md`.
- ✅ Audio players use `role="button"`, `tabindex="0"` and an `aria-label`, and respond to the keyboard (`js/audio-player.js:76, 163`).
- ✅ All images have alt text and all iframes have titles (the script raised no failures for either).

### Performance
- ⚠️ Several large images. `assets/tulip-blur.jpg` is 1.3 MB, `c0xy-squarespace-grab.jpg` 0.9 MB, and `moss-blur-light.jpg`, `meadow-blur-light.jpg` and `blured bg 2.jpg` are 0.6 MB each. Recompress them or convert to WebP/AVIF.
- ⚠️ Many `<img>` tags have no `width`/`height`, which can cause layout shift: covers, bear mascots, footer `bear-icon.png`, and the friends/laura/production photos. See the raw report for the list.
- ⚠️ Live cache headers are `public,max-age=0,must-revalidate` on CSS and assets, and `netlify.toml` has no `[[headers]]` block. Long-cache `assets/*` at least.
- ℹ️ Audio files are 3–7 MB (`moc-retail-sample.mp3` is 6.8 MB). They use `preload="metadata"` (`js/audio-player.js:130`), so they're fine on page load.
- ℹ️ Five assets aren't referenced anywhere but are still deployed: `blured bg 2.jpg`, `forest-blur.jpg`, `jenna-brown.jpg`, `meadow-blur-light.jpg` and one more (see raw).
- ✅ No 404s for any page or resource, locally or live. No console errors.
- ✅ All 13 player `data-src` MP3s return 200, and the players request their MP3s in the browser.

### Privacy
- ✅ On a fresh live load with no stored consent: no request to `googletagmanager.com` or `google-analytics.com`, and no `_ga` cookies.
- ✅ After Accept, `gtag/js` and `g/collect` load and the `_ga` and `_ga_SQPBS6DBKL` cookies are set.
- ✅ The footer "Cookies" button reopens the banner. Decline removes the `_ga` cookies, and the next page load makes no GA requests and shows no banner.
- ⚠️ YouTube iframes (`youtube.com/embed`) load before consent, at `index.html:256` and `work.html:311, 356`. They can set cookies. Use `youtube-nocookie.com` or a click-to-load facade.
- ⚠️ There's no privacy or cookie policy page, and neither the footer nor the banner links to one.
- ℹ️ Google Fonts are loaded from `fonts.googleapis.com`, which sends visitor IPs to Google. Self-hosting would avoid this.

### Security
